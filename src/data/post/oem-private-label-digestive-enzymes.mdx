## Who this is for

Brand owners, retailers, gym/spa buyers, and distributors evaluating **OEM / private label** or **wholesale** for digestive enzyme and probiotic dietary supplements.

## OEM vs wholesale

- **OEM / private label:** custom or standard formulas, capsule/powder formats, label and bottle artwork under your brand. Typical path: brief → sample → pilot → production.
- **Wholesale:** standard enzyme and probiotic SKUs in case packs for store shelves and wellness channels.

## Compliance note

Our materials use **dietary supplement structure/function** language only. We do **not** claim to diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent disease. Marketing copy must follow applicable labeling rules in your market.
